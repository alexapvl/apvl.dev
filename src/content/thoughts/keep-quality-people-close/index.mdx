title: "be kind and keep quality people close"
description: "a tyre shop, a copper paste habit and a website I built for free"
pubDate: 2026-11-23
tags: ["people", "kindness", "cars"]
---

around two years ago I started changing my tyres at a small shop called Vulcanix.
it was close to the gym I go to, so I saw the owner often, working inside the shop.

he is a simple guy.
but he treats every customer with the same respect, no matter what car they drive or what problem they show up with.
he is patient, he is friendly, and if you ask him what he is doing, he explains it.

I asked a lot.
I love cars, so I was curious what actually happens to a car before the new tyres go on.

one of those answers stuck with me.

before mounting a wheel, he cleans the hub with a wire brush on an impact gun, then puts a thin layer of copper paste on it.
that way the rim does not seize to the hub, and the next tyre change is easy.

he told me he does it for whoever does the next change.
not because he expects it to be him.

of course, it usually is him.
when you do work like that, people come back.
that is how a lot of his customers became regulars, myself included.

I loved that mentality.
I sent my mother to him, then my father.
my father had some bad rims that needed inspecting and replacing, and he helped him with them more than once.

the last time my father came back from the shop, he told me that Csobi, the owner, has some health problems and will need surgery and time off work.

I was sad to hear it.
and it made me wonder: what could I do to make this guy's day a tiny bit better?

one thing I know for sure is that I love building websites and making the people around me happy.

so I looked at his website.
it was a WordPress template that did not look anything like his shop.
no logo, no personality, information all over the place, photos that were not his, some of them made with AI. I hate seeing that.

I knew I had to help him.

I put together a small MVP over the weekend.
the plan was to bring my mother's car in for winter tyres on Saturday, show him the site and offer it to him, free.
he deserved to get something back for all that quality work.

he was very surprised.
he even wanted to pay me for it, even though I had never asked for anything.
that is when I knew I had picked the right person.

after some convincing on my side, I got away with a promised bottle of red wine and free tyre changes for my car.
he offered to do my mother's and grandmother's cars for free too.
I told him he can do that when they build him a website as well :)))

I did not want to get anything out of it.
just a smile on the face of a man whose name I did not even know until the day I showed him the website.

the site is live now at [vulcanix.ro](https://vulcanix.ro).

I try to do a good deed for someone every day.
this was one of those days.

and I think the lesson is the same one he taught me with the copper paste:
do good work for whoever comes next, without expecting it back.
the good people tend to stay close when you do.

thank you, Csobi, for putting your trust in me.
get well soon, the shop is not the same without you.
